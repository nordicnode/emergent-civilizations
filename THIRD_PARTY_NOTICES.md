# Third-party notices

## font8x8

The in-game character atlas `spr_df_font` is derived from the public-domain
font8x8 bitmap font by Daniel Hepper, based on Marcel Sondaar's 8x8 VGA font
work:

- https://github.com/dhepper/font8x8

The font is public domain. The atlas used here expands each 8x8 ASCII glyph
to a 16x16 tile with nearest-neighbour scaling.
